# Governance

Orvia uses a solo-maintainer model. [@9uiLe](https://github.com/9uiLe) is the maintainer and makes final decisions on scope, design, and releases.

- Proposals and changes are discussed in issues and pull requests.
- Significant architectural decisions are recorded as ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
- Pull requests to `master` require passing CI and resolved review conversations.
- Contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

If the project gains regular contributors, the maintainer may add co-maintainers and update this document to describe how decisions are made.
